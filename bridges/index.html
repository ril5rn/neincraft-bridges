Adjusted win %: how often a team would win if this player replaced an average player from the group, after accounting for who they played with and against. Ranked by this to start with: click any column heading to sort by it, and click again to flip the order.

Raw win %: actual wins ÷ games played. Skill: the rating behind the adjusted win % (0 = an average player from the group; the Team maker uses the 2026 one). On Last 30 days, Last 7 days and Today the small arrow next to it shows how much it changed compared with the earlier period (the same comparison as the vs prev / vs yesterday column). Games: team games played in the chosen time period.

vs prev / vs yesterday (Last 30 days, Last 7 days and Today): the change in adjusted win % compared with the period just before. Last 30 days and Last 7 days are compared with the same number of games played just before them (whatever their dates), and Today with yesterday's games (details above the table). The arrow next to a name shows how many places they have moved. It's blank for anyone who wasn't ranked back then, and the column only shows once someone was.

Tiers (adjusted): S 62%+ · A 53–62% · B 42–53% · C 30–42% · D under 30%.

Time period: every number is worked out again from just the games in that period, so a short period moves around more. Players need 10 games in the period to be ranked (3 for Today). Until then, someone from the group counts as exactly average in everyone else's numbers, and players from outside the group share one rating worked out from how they do. Min games hides anyone with fewer games than that in the period; it's remembered on this device.

Range: the same rating as the Skill board (the adjusted win %), drawn with how sure it is. The dot is the rating. The bar is the likely range: going by the games so far, the player's real level is inside it about 4 times out of 5.

More games make the bar shorter, so someone with few games has a wide one. When two players' bars overlap a lot, the games so far can't really tell them apart; when they don't overlap, the gap is real. The dashed line is an average player from the group (50%).

Click Likely range to sort by how wide it is. The time periods and Min games work as they do on the Skill board, except that there is no Today here (one day is too few games for a range). A shorter period has fewer games, so every bar gets wider and every rating is pulled closer to 50%.

Team maker

Tick who's playing and pick a team size. Every way to split them into two teams is listed from fairest to least fair, with each team's chance to win. If there are more players than places, it shows the fairest teams for each choice of who sits out.

The number next to each player is their skill: 0 is an average player from the group. A team's strength is its players' average skill, and the win chance comes from the gap between the two averages (the same model as the adjusted win %). A gap of 0.4 is about 60/40. Someone from the group who isn't ranked yet (under 10 games this year) is marked new and counts as average, 0.00.

Auto uses as many players as it can (7 ticked makes 3v3 with one sitting out). Teams are always the same size; an uneven game like 4v3 isn't suggested because the model can't tell how much the extra player is worth.

Skill comes from this year's team games across 2v2, 3v3 and 4v4, so players who are much better in one format than another may play a little above or below it. Your ticks and team size are remembered on this device.
